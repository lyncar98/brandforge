# Lautum — brand guidelines

The single source of truth for how Lautum looks and sounds. The
[`brandspec.json`](brandspec.json) encodes this for generation; this file is the
human-readable version. Lautum is an **AI governance control plane for sensitive,
regulated domains**. The brand should feel like serious AI infrastructure —
calm, precise, sovereign — in the register of OpenAI, Anthropic, Linear, and X.

## 1. Core principle

**Crisp and digital. Zero texture.** Every asset reads as if it were drawn by a
rendering engine, not photographed or illustrated. No grain, no film, no paper,
no noise, no halftone, no painterly anything.

## 2. Color — strictly grayscale

- The brand is **monochrome**. Pure black `#000`, near-white `#f5f5f5`, and the
  greys between them for depth. **Never** introduce a hue.
- Two surfaces, both valid (they mirror the product's dark/light themes):
  - **Dark** (default / hero): deep black field `#000`, luminous near-white lines.
  - **Light** (tiles / docs): off-white field `#f4f5f7`, near-black lines.
- Status colors (green/amber/red) exist in the product as a **UX signal only**.
  They are never brand and never appear in brand imagery.

## 3. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | **Archivo** (700–800) | headlines, wordmark; tight tracking (−0.4 to −1) |
| Body / UI | **Manrope** (400–700) | paragraphs, labels |
| Mono | **Spline Sans Mono** | kickers (UPPERCASE, wide tracking), tags, code, node labels |

## 4. The two motifs

1. **The control plane** — large translucent rectangular **planes stacked in
   receding depth**. The visual argument that Lautum sits *underneath* and
   *governs* what runs on top.
2. **The evidence graph** — a **sparse** set of small circular nodes joined by
   thin straight edges. Used deliberately and lightly — never a dense tangle.

Imagery combines them quietly. One clear subject; lots of air around it.

## 5. Composition rules

- **Generous negative space.** Roughly half of any hero is empty, reserved for a
  headline. Calm over busy, always.
- **Precise, thin, even-weight linework.** Sharp corners, CAD-like accuracy.
- **One subject.** If it looks cluttered or chaotic, it's wrong — simplify.
- **A faint flat shadow** at most. No glow, bokeh, lens blur, or 3D shininess.

## 6. Do / Don't

**Do:** stacked translucent planes · sparse node graphs · thin geometric lines ·
deep black or clean off-white fields · wide empty margins · architectural calm.

**Don't:** color · grain/texture/noise/film/paper · gradients-as-decoration ·
glow/blur/bokeh · photoreal scenes · dense line tangles · drop-shadow drama ·
logos/watermarks/UI chrome · unintended text in abstract art.

## 7. Voice

Precise, sober, technical, confident. Audience: engineers, CISOs, and risk
leaders at regulated enterprises. No hype, no buzzwords, no "effortless
compliance," no "AI-powered AI governance." Every claim is backed by a concrete
mechanism. The throughline: **governance is generated as a byproduct of building
and running AI — not assembled by hand.** Lautum is a control plane, not
documentation.
